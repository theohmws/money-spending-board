## What would flip a no

- If I tried the statement import for a month and it filled in the categories correctly with almost no fixes, saving me real time every month.
- A plain explanation, in Thai, of exactly what is stored from my slips and statements, who can access it, and how to delete it all.
- Something that catches my slips automatically without me having to remember, or a reminder that only costs me a few seconds a day.
- A setup that takes a few minutes with clear screenshots, and a trial where my first K PLUS slip actually lands in the app without me touching anything.
- A plain explanation of where the slips are stored, who at the company can see them, and a way to delete everything I uploaded, shown before I use the free trial.
- Uploading one of my own K PLUS or BBL slips and my own card statement in the free trial and seeing both come through with the right amounts and categories.
- If it picked up the transactions without me doing a daily step, for example by reading the monthly statement I already get and filling it in automatically.
- Proof that statements are parsed on my own device and never leave it, plus a way to delete everything with one tap.
- Seeing a clear list of the banks and cards it reads, or finding that my own slip or statement comes out right during the free trial.
- If it combined my K PLUS, Bangkok Bank and credit-card spending in one view that my bank apps cannot show, and the trial proved the Shortcut saves me real typing.
- A setup that takes about five minutes, with a step-by-step guide that works on my first K PLUS screenshot, so I can see my slips appear in the app before the trial ends.
- A clear explanation of who can access my uploaded slips and statements, and a way to delete them, backed by a known company or a trusted recommendation.
