Money Spending Board: a budgeting web app for Thai and English users.
Free: track spending by hand, split income into needs / savings / wants (50/30/20), see monthly trend and month-to-month comparison charts.
Free 14-day trial of two paid features:
- Slip capture: a one-time iPhone Shortcut reads the text of your bank slip (K PLUS, Bangkok Bank, TTB) with iOS text recognition on your phone. Setup takes one time and a video guide is included. Only the transaction details are saved to your account, not the slip photo.
- Credit-card statement import: upload your card statement PDF, which is read in your browser, and the categories are filled in for you.
After the trial: 69 baht to keep both features.
