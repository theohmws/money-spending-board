Money Spending Board: a budgeting web app for Thai and English users.
Free: track spending by hand, split income into needs / savings / wants (50/30/20), see monthly trend and month-to-month comparison charts.
Free 14-day trial of two paid features:
- Slip capture: a one-time iPhone Shortcut uses iOS text recognition on your phone (OCR) to read the text of your bank slip (K PLUS, Bangkok Bank, TTB). Only the recognised text is sent to your account, not the slip image. Setup is one time and a video guide is included.
- Credit-card statement import: your card statement PDF is read in your browser and the categories are filled in for you.
Your financial data is stored encrypted in our database.
After the trial: 69 baht per month to keep both features.
